# Phase 7.3 — Visual Design System, Typography, Color & Premium Art Direction Master Design Specification

---

## 1. VISUAL DESIGN NORTH STAR

> *"Sentinel Risk Engine visually expresses institutional financial authority through quiet restraint, crisp monospace data alignment, and decisive risk communication—hiding machine-learning complexity behind a single focused screening instrument."*

---

## 2. ART DIRECTION & PALETTE

- **Art Direction**: Light-First Institutional Fintech.
- **Canvas**: Soft neutral off-white background (`bg-slate-50`).
- **Surfaces**: Pure crisp white cards (`bg-white`) with 1px subtle borders (`border-slate-200`).
- **Typography**: Dual-font system:
  - Sans-Serif (`Inter`): UI labels, body text, headings, action badges.
  - Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`): `transaction_id`, timestamps, account strings, bank codes, amounts, and probability figures.
- **Risk Colors**: Localized strictly to decision badges, percentage displays, and action cards (`Emerald`, `Amber`, `Coral Red`, `Maroon Red`).
